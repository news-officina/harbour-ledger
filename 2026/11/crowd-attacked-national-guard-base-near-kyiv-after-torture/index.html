Crowd attacked National Guard base near Kyiv after torture-chamber fakes

Police used tear gas after about 200 people attacked a National Guard facility. Officials say the building was housing, not a prison, and Polish-language outlets spread the false claims.

9 November 2026

About 200 people attacked officers, military personnel and vehicles outside a National Guard facility in Petropavlivska Borshchahivka near Kyiv on November 2. Police used tear gas and warning shots. Interior Minister Ivan Vyhivskyi said the crowd gathered after a fake medic livestreamed false claims of a torture chamber. The SBI opened a case over abuse of official powers. Three participants, including Valentyna Moloda, are remanded in custody without bail.

The monitoring outlet Spravdi reported more than 1.6 thousand Polish-language publications by the evening of 5 November. They carried fakes about a 'secret Azov prison', '40 hostages' and the torture of a 14-year-old boy. More than 120 of them came from Russian and Belarusian propaganda resources, including about forty items on the Polish Pravda site. Over 20 accounts linked to Konfederacja, a Polish political group, posted more than 150 publications on X and Facebook.

National Guard press officer Ruslan Muzychuk said all four injured servicemen walked to the ambulance on their own. Vyhyvskyi said the SBI will give the torture-chamber claims a legal assessment and that the National Guard leadership is running an internal investigation.

The building at the centre of the claims was not a prison, according to the 1st Corps of the National Guard 'Azov'. It served as temporary housing for military unit 3121: mobilized men waiting for assignment and soldiers held after leaving their unit without permission. No civilians were held there. Olha Reshetylova of the military ombudsman's office wrote on Facebook that a check had been ordered. She said all servicemen were safe and getting medical help.

Kyrylo Budanov, head of the Office of the President, has taken the situation under personal control. The Prosecutor General's Office and the State Bureau of Investigation will handle the case, he said. He promised punishment for those responsible.
